How to Care for a Monstera Plant

Learn how to keep your Monstera healthy and thriving with the right light, water, soil and everyday care.

G The Green Corner Team
October 20, 2026 5 min read

Monstera plants are popular indoor plants known for their large, beautiful leaves. With a few simple care habits, you can help your plant grow well and become a lovely part of your home.

1. Give your Monstera the right light

Monsteras generally grow best in bright, indirect sunlight. Place your plant near a window where it receives plenty of natural light without strong, direct sunshine for long periods.

If the room is too dark, growth may become slow. Observe your plant and adjust its position when needed.

2. Water when the soil needs it

Monsteras prefer soil that stays lightly moist but does not remain waterlogged. Check the top few centimetres of soil before watering.

If the soil still feels wet, wait a little longer. When the top layer has dried, water thoroughly and allow excess water to drain from the pot.

3. Choose suitable soil and a pot

Use a well-draining potting mix that allows air to reach the roots. A pot with drainage holes helps prevent excess water from collecting around the roots.

As your Monstera grows, check whether its roots need more room. Repotting may be necessary when roots become crowded.

4. Keep the leaves clean

Dust can collect on large leaves and make them look dull. Gently wipe the leaves with a soft, damp cloth. Check the undersides regularly for signs of pests.

5. Watch for common plant problems

Yellow leaves, drooping and slow growth can have different causes. Check the soil moisture, lighting conditions, drainage and leaves before deciding what needs to change.

Avoid changing everything at once. Make adjustments gradually and observe how your plant responds.

A simple plant-care reminder

Every plant and home is different. Check your Monstera regularly and adjust your care routine according to its growing conditions.

Final thoughts

Caring for a Monstera does not have to be complicated. Suitable light, careful watering, well-draining soil and regular observation provide a good foundation for healthy growth.
