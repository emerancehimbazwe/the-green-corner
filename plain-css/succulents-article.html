How to Keep Your Succulents Healthy

The Green Corner · 5 minute read

Succulents are popular plants because they store water in their leaves and come in many beautiful shapes. With the right light, soil, and watering routine, they can thrive in your home.

1. Give them enough light

Most succulents need plenty of bright light. Place yours near a bright window and rotate the pot occasionally so all sides receive light. Introduce plants to stronger direct sunlight gradually to help prevent leaf damage.

2. Water only when needed

Let the soil dry out between waterings. When it is time to water, soak the soil thoroughly and allow excess water to drain away. Avoid leaving the pot sitting in water.

3. Choose well-draining soil

Use a potting mix made for cacti and succulents. Ordinary soil can hold too much moisture, which may cause root problems. Choose a pot with drainage holes.

4. Check your plant regularly

Look for changes in the leaves. Soft, yellowing leaves can indicate excess moisture, while wrinkled leaves may mean the plant needs water. Check the soil before changing your routine.

5. Be patient with growth

Succulents often grow slowly, especially during cooler or darker months. Keep their care consistent and avoid moving them frequently between very different environments.

Final tip

The best succulent-care routine starts with bright light, suitable soil, and careful watering. Observe your plant and adjust its care to the conditions in your home.
